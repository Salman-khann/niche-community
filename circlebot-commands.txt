CircleBot Commands Reference
===========================

Leaderboard and Analytics
-------------------------
/leaderboard
  Shows leaderboard table (top members by reputation + top communities by score).

/digest
  Posts community weekly digest summary.

Moderation and Safety
---------------------
/modlog
  Shows recent moderation log entries.
  Note: Admin/Moderator only.

/safety-status
  Shows current safety snapshot (flagged content counts + risk level).

/raid-mode on
/raid-mode off
  Toggles raid mode for the community.
  Note: Admin only.

Events and Onboarding
---------------------
/event-remind
  Posts reminder for the nearest upcoming event.

/event-remind <eventId>
  Posts reminder for a specific event.

/welcome-setup
  Posts onboarding/setup guidance for the community.

Content Curation
----------------
/bestof
  Shows best posts of the week based on engagement.

/unanswered
  Shows recent posts with no replies/comments.

Support and Utility
-------------------
/faq <topic>
  Shows help for a topic.
  Topics: invites, reputation, events, moderation, premium

/poll Question | Option 1 | Option 2 | [Option 3...]
  Creates a quick poll-style bot message.

Examples
--------
/faq reputation
/poll Which feature first? | Leaderboard | Digest | Event reminders
/event-remind 67f2b2a8c4b4bd8f83aa98d1
