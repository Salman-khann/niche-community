Niche Community Platform (Invite-Only)
Product Codename:

CircleCore
Architecture:
 MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express, React/Next.js, Node.js)
Category:
 Vertical Social / Community SaaS
Launch Focus:
 Invite-only professional, hobbyist, or interest-based communities
1️
⃣
 Executive Summary
Vision
A private, invite-only community platform that fosters 
trust, meaningful participation, and high-quality engagement
 for curated groups (e.g., founders, artists, researchers, niche fandoms, industry cohorts).
Value Proposition
“An invite-only social platform built for 
deep engagement, quality content, and meaningful connections
 — not noise.”
Features prioritized:
Strong identity & reputation systems
Invite codes and gated signups
Community discovery & interest matching
Multi-format content (posts, events, resources)
Moderation & trust signals
Paid membership tiers (optional)
